Nightly Grok Bot research 2026-11-08. Top 3 shipping patterns that fit Never 86'd ops intelligence and restaurant vertical.

1. Chief of Staff + specialist teams are the real shipping pattern. SpaceXAI engineer Lauren Tan runs 15+ Grok Bots (one CoS, three managers, 11 workers) that handle about 90% of routine engineering via verification loops and pstack skills so she reviews instead of writing. Users copy the same CoS router for ops, research, and content. Sources: Lauren Tan talks and X threads such as https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NDkvYEy_Bq0 and recent posts by Morlex and others.

2. Restaurant vertical plays with measurable ops results. Anthony Battaglia (Suite 200 Grille and Whiskey & Water) ships a CoS plus specialists for reservations, outreach, purchasing, bookkeeping, finance, and ops; the team caught up eight months of books, found about $28k in errors, and cut bookkeeper costs $1500/month while keeping standing approval rules. Brian Roemmele's phone-reservation bot (find number, confirm, dial after yes) went viral. RhinoAgents ships a master GM agent with nine sub-agents for phone hostess, table turns, inventory spoilage, labor, and closeout integrated to Toast, OpenTable, and 7shifts. Sources: https://aibusinessplays.com/plays/grok-bot-restaurant-books-caught-up-28k-errors/ , https://www.rhinoagents.com/ai-agents/restaurants , Roemmele X posts.

3. Public directories and enterprise-scale fleets. Brian Roemmele's GrokBot.Rocks tracks nearly 10k public bots; a large company he works with will release 300+ internal Grok Bots in working groups. Token limits are forcing governance shifts and tighter approval gates. Sources: https://i10x.ai/news/grokbot-rocks-directory-company-bots and Roemmele posts on X.

New capability that changes orchestration: Bots can learn from each other and hand work on a shared computer; Grok itself recommends a strict checker/reviewer before release. Token limits and death-loop failures (delegation collapse, usage burn with zero output) mean we should add explicit proof steps, Friday review routines, and least-privilege tools rather than more bots. Verification that agents can show evidence is the trust unlock Lauren and others highlight.
